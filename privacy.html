Sage

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026. This applies to Sage Men and Sage Women.

Overview

Sage is built to keep your data yours. There are no accounts, and we don't sell or share your personal information.

What stays on your device

Everything you track (habits, entries, journal, the feelings you name, workouts, fitness tests, sleep, measurements, reflections and settings) is stored on your device. If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS; Sage never sees it.

Medicines, supplements, urges and people

The supplements and medicines you list and the urges you ride out with the urge tool are stored on your device, in your backup export, and in your own iCloud if sync is on; what you tick off each day stays on your device. None of it is sent to us. Reminders for them say only the habit's name ("Time for your medicine"), never what you take. The people you add to the People tool, whether they're in your circle, and when you were last in touch, are stored with your profile, and in iCloud if sync is on. None of it is used for analytics; usage events only say that a tool was used.

iCloud sync

If you have Sage Premium and turn on iCloud sync, your data is also stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit. Only your Apple ID can read it; we can't. Cycle days, check-ups, your birth year, life stage and the feelings you name are also stored with CloudKit encryption, so only your devices can read them. Data imported from Apple Health is never synced. Reset in Settings deletes everything on your device and can also delete Sage's data from your iCloud (your other devices remove their copies when they next sync).

Apple Health

If you turn on Import from Health in Settings, Sage reads your steps, sleep, weight, exercise minutes, mindful minutes and time in daylight from Apple Health to fill in the habits you track. Imported data stays on the device that imported it: it isn't synced to iCloud, sent to us, or shared with anyone, and it is never used for advertising or marketing. If you turn on Save to Health, the workouts, meditation and breathwork sessions, sleep, weight and body fat you log in Sage are saved to Apple Health on your device. You can turn off access any time in the Health app.

Cycle tracking

If your version of Sage offers cycle tracking and you turn it on, the period flow, symptoms and notes you log stay on your device and, with iCloud sync, in your private iCloud database, where they're stored with CloudKit encryption so only your devices can read them. They're never sent to us, never used for analytics, crash reports or advertising, and never shared. The optional heads-up before a likely period never says what it's about. Period flow can be read from and saved to Apple Health if you allow it; flow imported from Health stays on your device. Predictions are estimates and aren't a form of birth control. Your life stage and its date (such as a due date) are stored the same way, encrypted. The optional daily reminder only says "Your daily reminder". Nothing about your cycle or life stage reaches usage statistics, not even that you logged a day or turned cycle tracking on. The report for your doctor is made on your device and goes only where you choose to share it; your notes are never included, and your name only if you add it. Turning cycle tracking off keeps what you've logged. Delete All Cycle Data (on the Cycle tab and in Settings) removes every logged day, your life stage and the daily reminder from your device and from iCloud, and what Sage saved to Apple Health; Reset deletes it too.

Health check-ups

If your version of Sage offers check-ups, the year you were born, the dates of the check-ups you log and anything you add about who you saw and what for stay on your device and, with iCloud sync, in your private iCloud database, where they're stored with CloudKit encryption so only your devices can read them. They're never sent to us, never used for analytics, crash reports or advertising, and never shared. Reminders only say "A check-up is due", never which, and usage statistics never include check-ups. Fitness test results are stored like your other workouts.

Accountability partner

If you add an accountability partner, their name and the phone number or email you give are stored with the rest of your data (and in iCloud if sync is on). Picking them from Contacts shares only the one entry you choose; Sage doesn't read your contacts. Check-ins and slip messages open in Messages for you to send, so they go from you, through Apple, to the person you chose, never to us. They include only the habits you pick (each one's goal, the days you met it, how close you got and your average) and leave out anything imported from Apple Health.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Live Activities

So Siri, Shortcuts and Control Center can log your habits, Sage shares your habit names with iOS on your device; Apple handles that under its own privacy policy, and it never reaches us. Widgets and Live Activities you add show your habits and timers on your Home Screen and Lock Screen; widgets never show your setbacks, and the urge tool never shows a Live Activity.

Crash reports and usage data

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to find bugs and see how the app is used. They collect crash reports, diagnostic logs, device and app version details, and general usage events, tied to a random ID created on install, not to your name or Apple ID. Usage events say which screens you open and which features you use, such as logging a built-in habit, finishing a workout or starting a challenge, with counts and lengths like how many sets or minutes. They never include anything you type (a custom habit's name, journal entries, notes, book titles), your accountability partner, or anything from Apple Health. They never say which setback you log or are quitting (every setback is just "a setback"), what triggered a slip, or anything about your cycle, check-ups, sex life or medicines. Crash reports include the screen you were on and a short log of recent actions. We don't use any of it for advertising, and advertising identifiers are switched off. You can turn usage data and crash reports off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, your message, which Sage app it's from, the app version and the time are sent to us through Formspree. Don't include anything you'd rather we didn't see.

Who handles it, where, and for how long

Crash reports and usage data are processed for us by Google (Firebase) and feedback by Formspree, both in the United States, under their own privacy terms. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days and usage events for at most 14 months. We keep feedback only as long as we need it to reply and improve the app. Everything else you log stays with you and isn't held by us at all.

Your choices

You can turn off usage data and crash reports in Settings → Privacy, turn off Apple Health access in the Health app, export everything you've logged, and delete it all with Reset. Because usage data isn't tied to your name or Apple ID, we can't look up yours; to delete feedback you sent, email us from the address you'd like answered and say roughly when you sent it.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders, streak alerts, check-in nudges, check-up reminders and the cycle heads-up are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server to deliver them. Each can be turned off in Settings or where you set it (a yoga path's reminder on that path's page). Ones that could reveal something private (a setback, a check-up, your cycle, what you take) never say what they're about on your Lock Screen.

Exporting your data

Export Habit Log and Export Full Backup in Settings create files on your device for you to save or share. They include everything you've logged, Apple Health imports too, and go only where you send them.

Deleting your data

Reset All Data in Settings erases everything on this device and, if you choose, Sage's data in your iCloud too (your other devices then remove their copies). Deleting the app erases what's on the device. You can also remove synced data in your device's iCloud settings. To delete only your cycle data, use Delete All Cycle Data.

Children

Sage isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.

Contact
