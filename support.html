Sage

Support

For Sage Men and Sage Women. The quickest way to reach us is Settings → Support → Send Feedback in the app, or email sage.app.feedback@gmail.com.

Restore a purchase

Open Sage Premium (You → Sage Premium) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Move to a new iPhone

With Sage Premium, turn on iCloud sync in Settings → iCloud. On the new iPhone, tap "Used Sage before? Restore from iCloud" on the first screen. Without Premium, use Settings → Your Data → Export Full Backup, then Restore from Backup on the new phone.

Your data and privacy

There are no accounts. What you log stays on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud. Cycle data, check-ups and health details are encrypted there so only your devices can read them. Read the Privacy Policy.

Delete your data

Settings → Reset All Data erases everything on your iPhone and can also delete Sage's data in your iCloud. In Sage Women, Your Cycle Data → Delete All Cycle Data removes only your cycle data.

Not medical advice

Sage is a habit tracker, not a medical device. Cycle predictions are estimates and aren't birth control, and check-up timings are general guidance. Talk to your doctor about your health. See the Terms of Use.
